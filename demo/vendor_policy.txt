Vendor Security Policy — Acme Cloud (internal)

1. Data retention
Customer personal data is retained for 18 months after contract end, then deleted from primary stores.

2. Encryption
Encryption at rest is required for all customer databases. AES-256 is the baseline. TLS 1.2 or newer is required in transit.

3. Vendor access
Production access by vendors is forbidden unless a numbered change ticket is approved by Security. Shared root passwords are not allowed.

4. Logging
Access to production must be logged. Logs are kept for 90 days.

5. Incidents
Customers are notified of a confirmed personal-data incident within 72 hours.
