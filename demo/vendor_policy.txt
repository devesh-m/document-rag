ACME CLOUD — VENDOR SECURITY POLICY
Internal use. Version 3.2
Effective date: 12 March 2026
Owner: Security Operations

This policy applies to every vendor that stores, processes, or can reach Acme Cloud customer data.

1. Data retention
Customer personal data is retained for 18 months after contract end, then deleted from primary stores. Backups are purged within 30 days of that deletion. Retention exceptions require a written Legal ticket and a named expiry date.

2. Encryption
Encryption at rest is required for all customer databases. AES-256 is the baseline. TLS 1.2 or newer is required in transit. Unencrypted disks are not permitted in production. Encryption keys are stored in the company KMS and rotated at least every 12 months.

3. Vendor access
Production access by vendors is forbidden unless a numbered change ticket is approved by Security. Shared root passwords are not allowed. Vendor sessions must use SSO and expire after 8 hours. After the ticket closes, access must be revoked the same business day.

4. Logging
Access to production must be logged. Logs are kept for 90 days and must include actor, action, and timestamp. Vendors may not disable or redact production audit logs.

5. Incidents
Customers are notified of a confirmed personal-data incident within 72 hours. A written post-incident report is due within 10 business days. The report must name the systems affected and the data classes involved.

6. Subprocessors
Vendors may not add a subprocessor that can read customer personal data without 30 days' written notice to Acme Security.
